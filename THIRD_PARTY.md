# Third party assets

- This travel site's China display groups Taiwan and the South Tibet claim area with China. The regional geometry is derived from Natural Earth ne_10m_admin_0_countries and ne_10m_admin_0_disputed_areas.
- Current weather is provided by Open-Meteo. https://open-meteo.com/

- Three.js 0.170.0, MIT license. https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r170/LICENSE
- Land and country polygons from Natural Earth ne_110m_land and ne_110m_admin_0_countries, public domain. Boundaries are generalized for globe display. https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
- Example photography: see images/credits.json. Bangkok photograph by Ninara, CC BY 2.0: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:4Y1A1159_Bangkok_(33536795515).jpg . Images may be cropped for presentation.

City coordinates represent approximate city centers, not private or trip-specific locations. Landmark meshes are original stylized geometric illustrations and are not to scale. Example photographs do not necessarily represent the city shown; replace `image` and optional `photos` fields in `data/cities.js` with personal photography.
